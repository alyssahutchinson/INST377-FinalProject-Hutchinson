Why We're Here

We know the days are busy and there's not much time to hit the grocery store. Sometimes, you just need ideas with the ingredients you already have. Maybe you want to try some new cuisine! Save-A-Plate is here to be that helpful tool you need to help reduce one stressor of mealtimes.



How to use Save-A-Plate: FAQs

Type in the main ingredient you want to use, and get results of meals with that ingredient as the main component, and what are in the world the meal is considered to be from.


Type in the category of meal you want (seafood, dessert, etc), and get results of meals within that category.


Fill out the 'Add to Favorites' form on the Homepage to add a meal to your favorites list for future reference.


Go to the 'Favorites' Page and scroll through your area makeup chart and the favorites list.
